Professional Communication Skills 1 course provided valuable exposure to academic and professional communication skills, particularly in report writing, critical reading, group discussions, and oral presentations. Throughout the course, skills in analysing academic texts, synthesizing information, paraphrasing, summarizing, and constructing systematic arguments were significantly strengthened through individual and group-based assessments. The report writing project enhanced critical thinking, research, and professional writing abilities while improving the use of formal language, cohesion, transition markers, and proper referencing techniques in academic writing. In addition, group discussions and oral presentations strengthened teamwork, communication, confidence, and presentation skills through collaborative analysis and presentation of researched topics to different audiences. The course also increased awareness of the importance of effective communication, critical analysis, and ethical writing practices in academic and professional environments. Overall, this course successfully enhanced both written and spoken communication skills while improving the ability to present ideas clearly, professionally, and systematically.
