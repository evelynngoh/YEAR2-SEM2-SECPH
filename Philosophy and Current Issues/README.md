Philosophy and Current Issues course provided valuable understanding of philosophy, national education philosophy, Rukun Negara, and contemporary societal issues in the Malaysian context. Throughout the course, knowledge of philosophical thinking, ethical values, national identity, Falsafah Pendidikan Kebangsaan (FPK), Rukun Negara, and Falsafah Sejahtera was significantly strengthened through lectures, student-centred learning activities, group discussions, and case study analysis. The course enhanced critical thinking, analytical reasoning, communication, and problem-solving skills by encouraging discussions on current issues such as unity, education, social harmony, ethics, globalization, and national development. In addition, exposure to the relationship between philosophy, education, and nation-building increased awareness of the importance of moral values, cultural understanding, and responsible citizenship in maintaining harmony within a multicultural society. Overall, this course successfully enhanced intellectual, ethical, and social awareness while encouraging holistic and balanced personal development based on the principles of FPK and Rukun Negara.
